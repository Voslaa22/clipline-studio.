01 Why it works

Why clips work for podcasts

A full episode asks for an hour. A clip asks for 30 seconds, then sends the people who liked it to the full show.

1.

The best part is buried at minute 52

Most people will never press play on a 90-minute episode. A clip puts the best moment in front of them first.

2.

Guest moments travel

Every strong guest moment is a clip your guest can share with their own audience, which puts your show in front of new listeners.

3.

One episode, a week of posts

A long conversation has plenty of standalone moments, so one recording can keep you posting daily until the next episode is out.

06 Questions

Things people ask before they start

Do you clip audio-only podcasts?

I work from video. If your show is audio-only, email me first and I'll tell you honestly whether clips will work for it.

How long are the clips?

Usually 20 to 60 seconds. Each clip is as long as the moment needs to make sense to someone who has never heard your show.

Can you clip older episodes?

Yes. Plans count clips, not episodes, so I can pull moments from your back catalogue as well as new episodes.

Do you post to my accounts, or do I?

Either. On Growth and Scale I post for you with scheduling access you grant. On Starter I deliver finished files and captions, and you post.

Who owns the clips?

You do. They're cut from your episodes and delivered to you. I never post your clips anywhere except the accounts you choose.
