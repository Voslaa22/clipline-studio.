Three steps, and only the first one is yours.

08 Questions

Things people ask before they start

Do you post to my accounts, or do I?

Either. On Growth and Scale I post for you with scheduling access you grant. On Starter I deliver finished files and captions, and you post.

Who owns the clips?

You do. They're cut from your videos and delivered to you. I never post your clips anywhere except the accounts you choose.

How do you pick the moments?

My pipeline scans the whole video for hooks, reactions, stories and payoffs, then scores them. Each clip must make sense to someone who has never seen your channel.

What if a video has nothing clip-worthy?

I'd rather post fewer good clips than padding. Unused clips roll over to the next video.

What access do you need to post for me?

On Growth and Scale you connect your accounts through each platform’s own business or creator tools, the same way you’d add a scheduling app. I never ask for your password, and you can revoke the access at any time. On Starter nothing is connected: you get files.

How fast do clips arrive?

The 5 free clips within 48 hours of your request. On a plan, clips from a new upload are usually ready within 48 hours and go out on the daily schedule; Scale gets priority turnaround.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time. Plans are month to month.
