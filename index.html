Questions

Do you post to my accounts, or do I?

Either. On Growth and Scale we post for you with scheduling access you grant. On Starter we deliver finished files and captions, and you post.

Who owns the clips?

You do. They're cut from your videos and delivered to you. We never post your clips anywhere except the accounts you choose.

How do you pick the moments?

Our pipeline scans the whole video for hooks, reactions, stories and payoffs, then scores them. Each clip must make sense to someone who has never seen your channel.

What if a video has nothing clip-worthy?

We'd rather post fewer good clips than padding. Unused clips roll over to the next video.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time. Plans are month to month.
