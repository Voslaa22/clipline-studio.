01 Why it works

Why Shorts work for long-form creators

Shorts are where new viewers sample your channel before they commit to a 20-minute video.

1.

A front door to your channel

A good Short shows what your channel is about in seconds and gives people a reason to watch the full video.

2.

Your back catalogue is full of clips

Videos you published months ago still have moments that work as Shorts today.

3.

Daily posts without filming more

One long video can fill a week of Shorts, so you post every day without adding shoots.

02 How it works

You create. I do the rest.

Every clip is reframed to 9:16, captioned word by word, gets a hook title for the first second and your branding.

Step 1

You upload as usual

Every new long video or episode on your channel goes straight into my clipping pipeline. Nothing to send, no extra work.

Step 2

I cut the moments

Hooks, reactions, stories and one-liners are picked, reframed to 9:16, captioned word by word and branded to your channel.

Step 3

They go out daily

Clips are scheduled across TikTok, Reels and Shorts, or delivered as ready-to-post files if you'd rather post yourself.

06 Questions

Things people ask before they start

Do you upload Shorts to my channel?

On Growth and Scale, yes, with access you grant. On Starter I deliver finished files and captions, and you post.

Can you clip videos that are already out?

Yes. Plans count clips, not videos, so I can pull moments from older uploads as well as new ones.

How long are the clips?

Usually 20 to 60 seconds. Each clip has to make sense to someone who has never seen your channel.

Who owns the clips?

You do. They're cut from your videos and delivered to you. I never post your clips anywhere except the accounts you choose.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time. Plans are month to month.
